🚪 STEP 1: The Compounder Pass (The True Gatekeeper)
Timing: 1:30 PM - 2:00 PM • Sit at Compounder's Desk

Psychology: Compounder doctor ka guard hai. Usko dar lagta hai ki naya system aane se uska kaam badhega. Dikhayiye ki isse uska subah ka jhagda khatam hoga.

"Namaste Bhaiya! Ek minute aapka chahiye tha. Aap subah 8 baje se itne marizon ko sambhalte hain, hum dekh rahe the kitna bheed aur kheenchtan hoti hai counter par.

Subah jo 50 mariz aakar aapse ladte hain ki 'Bhaiya mera number pehle lagao', aur aapko baar-baar chilla kar bolna padta hai—us pure jhanjhat ko khatam karne ke liye humne ek Free WhatsApp Token System banaya hai.

Clinic ke bahar ek chota sa QR placard lag jayega. Mariz aate hi phone se scan karega, uske WhatsApp par seedha token number #TK-12 chala jayega. Mariz aapse bahas hi nahi karega kyunki uske phone mein time aur number likha hoga.

Aur sabse badiya baat: Aapko koi computer ya typing nahi karni hai, sab mariz ke WhatsApp par automatic hota hai. Bhaiya, ek baar 1 minute ke liye doctor sahab ko dikha dein? Unka bhi 1 ghanta bachega aur clinic ka mahol shaant rahega."

💡 Result: Compounder khud bolega: "Sir, ek local bhaiya aaye hain, clinic ke token aur WhatsApp ke liye badiya cheez laya hai, 2 minute sun lijiye."

🩺 STEP 2: Doctor 30-Second Hook (The Anti-Sales Pitch)
First 30 Seconds in Doctor's Cabin

Psychology: Doctor thaka hua hai aur soch raha hai aap kuch bechne aaye hain. Pehle 10 second me unka darr khatam kijiye.

"Namaste Doctor Sahab! Sir, sabse pehle main clear kar doon:
Main na koi dawai bechne aaya hoon, na koi software bechne aaya hoon, aur aapse 1 rupiya bhi nahi chahiye.

Sirf 1 minute: Humne Line Bazar ke clinics ke liye ek Automated WhatsApp System banaya hai jo aapke do sabse bade sar-dard khatam kar deta hai:
1. OPD Token Rush: Subah mariz jo clinic ke bahar token ke liye ladte hain, unko seedha WhatsApp par token mil jata hai.
2. Late-Night Disturbance: Mariz jo raat ko aapke personal number par call/WhatsApp karke report bhejte hain, wo band ho jayega—clinic ke official WhatsApp par automate ho jayega.

Sir, iska setup 0 rupiya hai, doctor ke liye 100% Free hai, aur aapki 100% consultation fee seedhe aapke account mein aati hai. Ek live demo sirf aapke phone par 30 second mein dikha doon?"

🛡️ Top 4 Objections & Exact Counter-Punch
Guaranteed Answers
❓ Sawal 1: "Tumhara kya fayda hai? Free kyun de rahe ho? Baad me charge karoge?"
Jawab: "Sir, doctor ke liye yeh life-time 100% Free hai. Hamara model yeh hai ki jo mariz pharmacy delivery ya lab report mangwate hain, unse micro-convenience fee aati hai. Doctor ki consultation fee se hum 1 paisa bhi nahi kaat-te."
❓ Sawal 2: "Mujhe computer chalana nahi aata, mere paas time nahi hai."
Jawab: "Sir, aapko computer chhuna hi nahi hai! Aap jaise pen se parchi likhte hain, waise hi likhte rahiye. Yeh mariz ke phone aur reception par chalega. Aapka 1 second bhi barbad nahi hoga."
❓ Sawal 3: "Practo wale bhi aaye the, unka to bahut jhanjhat tha."
Jawab: "Sir, Practo mariz ko bolta hai 50MB ka app download karo aur aapse ₹3,000/month leta hai. Bihar me koi mariz app download nahi karta. Hum koi app nahi dete—sab kuch seedha WhatsApp par chalta hai jo har mariz ke phone me pehle se hai."
❓ Sawal 4: "Abhi nahi, baad me dekhenge / Card chhod jao."
Jawab (The 2-Day Urgency Close): "Sir bilkul. Hum Line Bazar me sirf shuru ke 3 clinics ko pilot launch me free priority placard de rahe hain. Main kal subah 10 baje aakar compounder ke desk par QR laga deta hoon. Sirf 2 din chala kar dekhiye—agar pasand na aaye toh turant hata dijiyega. Koi contract nahi hai."
🎯 The Chemist / Lab Backdoor Strategy (The Trojan Horse)
When Compounder Blocks You

1. Clinic ke bagal wale Medical Hall (Chemist) ke paas dopahar 2 baje jayiye.
2. Boliye: "Bhaiya, doctor sahab ke chronic mariz (BP, Sugar wale) 1 mahine baad dawai kisi aur dukan se le lete hain. Hum WhatsApp par yaad dilayenge ki 'Aapki dawai khatam ho rahi hai, 1-Click me deliver mangwayein'—mariz aapka permanent ho jayega."
3. Chemist ko bolen: "Bhaiya, doctor sahab ko chal kar sath me dikha dete hain."
4. Chemist doctor ka dost hota hai. Chemist aapko cabin me le jayega, aur doctor apne chemist ki baat 100% sunega!
